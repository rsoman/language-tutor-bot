# D0003: Nouns show the grammatical features their language has

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Canvas:** https://claude.ai/artifact/CFP8hVHwBhNRCC9CJwppkN (Main: Vocabulary tab, Mobile: flashcards and word cards)
- **Supersedes:** none

## Rule

Which noun features the vocabulary views show (sheet, flashcards, word help) depends on the
language, and each `LanguageProfile` declares them. For example:

- German declares article, gender and plural, so a noun always appears with its article
  ("der Tisch") and its plural.
- English has no gendered articles, so the sheet has no article or gender column.
- A language with measure words or noun classes could declare those instead.

The UI builds its columns from what the profile declares, never from a fixed list.
Feature values such as plurals are stored per word and never generated by a rule, because they are often irregular.

## Why

What a learner must memorise with a noun differs by language. Hard-coding article and gender
would be wrong for many languages and would break the language-agnostic rule.
