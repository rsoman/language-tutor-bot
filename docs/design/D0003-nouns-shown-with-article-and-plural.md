# D0003: Nouns are always shown with their article and plural

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Canvas:** https://claude.ai/artifact/CFP8hVHwBhNRCC9CJwppkN (Main: Vocabulary tab, Mobile: flashcards and word cards)
- **Supersedes:** none

## Rule

Wherever a noun appears in vocabulary views (sheet, flashcards, word help), it is shown with its
article and its plural form. Plurals are stored per word, never generated by a rule.
These fields are nullable, so languages without articles or grammatical gender leave them empty
and the UI hides the columns.

## Why

Article and plural are part of learning a noun in gendered languages, and plural forms are often irregular.
